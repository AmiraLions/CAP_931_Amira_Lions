# FreedomFlyer — Reviewed Sales Brief

## Prospect and target contact
Sunshine State RVs in Gainesville, Florida, is the prospect for the fictional proposed FreedomFlyer motorhome line. The dealership describes itself as a Class B specialist offering new and pre-owned vehicles. Its staff page identifies target contact Nick Schmidt as General Manager/Owner [S1] [S2].

## Company strategy
The dealership says it chooses brands for quality and reputation and seeks long-term customer relationships. Its website highlights financing, maintenance, customization, and free delivery. These statements suggest a conversation about product support and customer experience, but do not independently establish demand for electric motorhomes [S1].

## Competitors and positioning
Retrieved dealership entries explicitly marked NEW and Class B include Coachmen Beyond 22D AWD Li3 and Midwest Ford Patriot MD2F AWD [S3]; Roadtrek Chase and Tiffin GH1 SE 19SAV [S4]; and Winnebago Solis Pocket 36A [S5]. These examples establish new-listing relationships without implying exclusive partnerships or sales volume.

Noovo Lite is marked NEW and COMING SOON; Noovo Next AWD is marked NEW with Pre-Order availability [S4]. A separate 2026 Noovo NEXT entry is also marked NEW [S6]. These qualifications matter: a new listing does not necessarily mean a vehicle is immediately available.

FreedomFlyer's proposed differentiation is electric luxury Class B travel with zero tailpipe emissions and expandable solar designed to support beautiful advantures. These are fictional design targets requiring engineering validation, not demonstrated production capabilities. The evidence does not establish whether this proposition would meet the dealership's commercial requirements.

## Leadership and relevant reports
Schmidt's title is verified, but his specific purchasing authority is not independently established by the staff page [S2]. No relevant corporate filing or formal strategy document was supplied in this run; that limitation does not establish that such documents do not exist.

## Proposed conversation angle and two discovery questions
Hypothesis: Explore whether a proposed electric camper van could complement the dealer's documented Class B offering. Discuss service readiness, charging support, warranty responsibilities, and buyer needs before assuming a fit. Present FreedomFlyer as a concept to evaluate rather than an available vehicle.

1. What criteria and approval process determine whether you add a new Class B manufacturer?
2. What customer inquiries about electric propulsion or expanded solar capability have you received, and what support would you require?
